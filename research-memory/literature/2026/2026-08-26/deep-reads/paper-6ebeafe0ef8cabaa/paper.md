# Dual‐Metal Synergy in Fe 2 Nb: Regulating D‐p Orbital Overlap to Boost Polyiodide Redox and Suppress Shuttle Effect in Aqueous Zinc‐Iodine Batteries

## 摘要与研究价值

**Original:** ABSTRACT Aqueous zinc‐iodine batteries (AZIBs) suffer from polyiodide shuttle, sluggish redox kinetics, and poor mechanical stability that impede their application in flexible energy storage. Herein, we report a multi‐scale confinement‐catalysis synergy enabled by embedding ultrafine Fe 2 Nb bimetallic alloy nanoclusters (2–6 nm) into a three‐dimensional microporous carbon matrix (FeNb@C). The framework is fabricated via supramolecular self‐assembly of an F127/4,4'‐bipyridine/Cu 2 + /Co 2 + coordination polymer, followed by carbonization‐etching and in situ impregnation‐thermal reduction. Density functional theory computations confirm that the Fe 2 Nb alloy catalyst achieves the optimal trade‐off between adsorption confinement and redox reversibility and that dual‐metal synergy of Fe 2 Nb can regulate d‐p orbital overlap of the FeNb alloy and polyiodide to boost polyiodide redox and suppress the shuttle effect, enabling superior charge–discharge reversibility and energy efficiency, which renders it the most promising catalytic host for aqueous zinc‐iodine batteries. Consequently, the as‐obtained FeNb@C/I 2 cathode maintains a capacity of 200.3 mAh g − 1 after 5200 cycles with a high capacity retention of 97.2%. Mechanistic studies by ex situ Raman/UV–vis and post‐mortem Zn‐anode analyses verify complete I 3 − →I 2 re‐conversion and suppressed polyiodide shuttling. A 5 × 7 cm 2 flexible pouch cell exhibits 2 for wearable electronics.

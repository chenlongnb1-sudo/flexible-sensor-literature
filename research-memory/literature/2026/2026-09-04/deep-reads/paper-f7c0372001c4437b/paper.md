# Simultaneously Optimize Thermoelectric Parameters in Carbon Nanotube Composites: The Pivotal Role of Diradical Spiro Molecules

## 摘要与研究价值

**Original:** ABSTRACT Organic thermoelectric materials hold immense promises for wearable electronic devices, offering the potential to harness body heat for power generation. However, the interdependence of thermoelectric performance parameters, resulting in relatively low overall performance, remains a major limitation for power generation applications. In this study, a series of novel diradical spiro molecules are synthesized and integrated with single‐wall carbon nanotubes (SWCNTs) as p ‐ and n ‐type thermoelectric materials. Diradical spiro molecules leverage synergistic advantages of radical structures and spiro cross‐conjugated backbones. Radical structures align molecular energy levels with SWCNTs, facilitating electron transfer. Spiro cross‐conjugated backbone strengthens molecular adsorption on SWCNTs, achieving excellent dispersion and strong interactions in carbon nanotube networks. This dual strategy enables simultaneous optimization of three key thermoelectric parameters, leading to significant enhancement in thermoelectric performance. The SFX2‐PO •• /SWCNT composites, where the SFX2‐PO •• molecule possesses a spiro[dibenzo[c,h]xanthene‐7,9′‐fluorene] (SFX2) core and bis(2,6‐di‐ tert ‐butylphenoxy radical) (PO •• ) substituents, exhibit the highest power factors among both p ‐ and n ‐type materials, attributed to its highest doping level and strongest interaction energy. The corresponding thermoelectric generators show reliable sensitivity to low‐grade heat sources such as human skin, together with mechanical, thermal, and air stability, underscoring their strong potential for wearable and portable energy harvesting applications.

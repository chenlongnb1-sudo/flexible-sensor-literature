# Enhanced Performance and Stability of Paper‐Based Cs 3 Cu 2 I 5 Photodetectors via PVK In Situ Encapsulation

- 期刊：Advanced Optical Materials
- 日期：2026-09-05
- DOI：10.1002/adom.71744

## 摘要与研究价值

**Original:** ABSTRACT The quest for resilient photodetectors in flexible electronics is met with the challenge of perovskite instability under mechanical stress and environmental conditions. This study introduces a Cs 3 Cu 2 I 5 /poly(N‐vinylcarbazole) (PVK) photodetector on paper, where PVK not only encapsulates Cs 3 Cu 2 I 5 to shield it from environmental degradation but also synergistically enhances photoconductive properties. The device exhibits a responsivity (R) of 1.35 A/W, remains stable operation after 5000 bending cycles, and retains 75% of its initial performance following 215 days of exposure.

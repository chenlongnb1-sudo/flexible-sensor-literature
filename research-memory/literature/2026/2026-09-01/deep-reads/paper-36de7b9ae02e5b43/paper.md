# Electrically Conductive but Thermally Insulating PPy‐Coated Nonwoven Fabric for Wearable Thermal Management, Electromagnetic Interference Shielding, and Sensing Applications

- 期刊：Advanced Functional Materials
- 日期：2026-08-30
- DOI：10.1002/adfm.77952
- 解析状态：metadata_only_no_legal_pdf

## 摘要与研究价值

**Original:** ABSTRACT Flexible wearable electronics in the 5G/6G era require multifunctional fabrics integrating electromagnetic interference (EMI) shielding, sensing, electro‐thermal conversion, and thermal insulation, yet these properties are inherently incompatible. Herein, we modified the surface of a 2D network in non‐woven fabric (NWF) by doping Cl– into PPy (NWF‐P‐X) through a simple controllable layer‐by‐layer immersing–polymerization process. The introduction of Cl – ‐doped PPy shell constructed conductive networks within 2D NWF matrices, resulting in boosted electrical conductivity ( σ ) (65.13 S/m), electro‐thermal conversion efficiency, and pressure‐responsive sensing performance. The high σ endowed the NWF‐P‐X with great wide‐frequency absorption‐dominated EMI shielding effectiveness (EMI SE). The SET was beyond 25 dB (99.68377% shielding capacity) over 30 MHz–4.5 GHz and beyond 40 dB (99.99% shielding) over 8.0–40.0 GHz. The NWF‐P‐X also presented a rapid heat response under a low voltage (4.0 V) and a wide temperature range (29.6°C–107.4°C). Moreover, the NWF‐P‐X exhibited superior thermal insulation performance with low thermal conductivity (0.5537 W∙m −1 ∙K −1 in‐plane; 0.0650 W∙m −1 ∙K −1 out‐of‐plane), where the porous structure and phase interfaces critically enable multifunctional compatibility for EMI protection, thermal management, and sensing applications.

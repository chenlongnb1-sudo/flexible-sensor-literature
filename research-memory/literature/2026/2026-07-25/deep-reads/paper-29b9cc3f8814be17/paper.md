# 3D Printed Flexible Piezoelectric Self‐Charging Supercapacitors for Self Powered Electronics

## 摘要与研究价值

**Original:** ABSTRACT The increasing demand for autonomous power systems in wearable and smart electronics has driven the development of integrated devices capable of simultaneous energy harvesting and storage. Here, a flexible piezoelectric self‐charging supercapacitor (PSSC) is developed by integrating a high‐performance supercapacitor with a direct ink writing (DIW)‐fabricated three‐dimensional PVDF‐ZnO piezoelectric architecture. The supercapacitor, based on binder‐free MnO 2 nanosheets grown on conductive carbon fiber cloth, delivers an areal capacitance of 0.86 F cm − 2 at 2 mA cm − 2 , with an energy density of 0.119 Wh cm − 2 and a power density of 1 W cm − 2 . It also exhibits excellent electrochemical durability, retaining 94.01% capacitance after 10,000 cycles, along with stable performance under repeated mechanical deformation. The DIW‐fabricated piezoelectric structure acts as both a separator and energy harvesting layer, generating a piezopotential under mechanical deformation that drives ion migration and charges the supercapacitor to a stored voltage of up to 866 mV. The three‐dimensional architecture enhances strain‐induced polarization, electrolyte accessibility, and ion transport, thereby improving electromechanical‐electrochemical coupling. These results suggest that the proposed PSSC is a viable flexible power unit for future wearable electronic applications.
